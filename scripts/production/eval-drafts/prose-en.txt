Treatment: The Lighthouse Keeper

The story opens in the keeper's kitchen at night. Mara, an old woman, sits alone with a cold cup of tea. A storm rattles the shutters. She looks at the empty chair across from her and says it is late, and that he should have been home hours ago.

Later, on the narrow stairs of the lighthouse, Mara climbs towards the lamp with a lantern in her hand. Halfway up she stops to listen. Somewhere above her a door bangs in the wind.

At dawn the storm has passed. On the rocks below the tower, Mara finds her husband's boat, unharmed, tied where he always leaves it. She laughs quietly, and then she cries.

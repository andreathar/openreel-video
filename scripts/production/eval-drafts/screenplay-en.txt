THE LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER

INTERIOR. KITCHEN - NIGHT

Rain hammers the shutters. MARA (70s) sits at a small table with a cold cup of tea.

MARA
It's late. He should be home by now.

She stares at the empty chair.

                                                    2.

TOM
(from the doorway)
The boat is fine. Stop worrying about it.

MARA (CONT'D)
I will worry when I like.

CUT TO:

INTERIOR. STAIRWELL - NIGHT

Mara climbs the narrow stairs with a lantern. Halfway up she stops to listen.

MARA
Is anyone there?
(MORE)

INTERIOR. LAMP ROOM - DAWN

The storm has passed. Pale light fills the glass.

TOM
Look at that.
